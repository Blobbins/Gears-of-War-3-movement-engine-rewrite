    Controls

    Stick
    Left stick, or WASD / arrow keys
    A
    Controller A, or Space
    Tap A
    Slide into cover in reach, else evade
    Hold A
    Roadie run (after 0.3 s); into a wall = cover
    In cover
    Pull the stick away from the wall to push off it. At an edge: stick toward the wall + A = cover slip, stick along the wall + A = swat turn to cover across a gap (else evade out). Tap A again during a swat and hold it = roadie run out of it. Hold A = run along cover. On waist-high cover: stick toward the wall + A = vault over
    R
    Reset

    What's real here

    Speeds, timings, reach distances and which moves can chain come from Gears 3's own code and settings. Slip and evade distances are placeholders until the animations are exported. Walls are drawn but not yet solid for walking. Vaults aren't built yet.
