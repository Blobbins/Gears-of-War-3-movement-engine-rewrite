    Controls

    Move
    Left stick, or WASD
    Look
    Right stick, mouse (click the view), or arrow keys
    A
    Controller A, or Space
    Tap A
    Slide into cover in reach, else evade
    Hold A
    Roadie run (after 0.3 s); into a wall = cover
    In cover
    Stick left / right moves along the wall. Pull the stick away from the wall to push off it. At an edge: stick toward the wall + A = cover slip, stick along the wall + A = swat turn to cover across a gap (else evade out). Tap A again during a swat and hold it = roadie run out of it. Hold A = run along cover. On waist-high cover: stick toward the wall + A = vault over (keep A held to roadie run out of the landing)
    Aim
    Left trigger, or right mouse (walks at half speed, no roadie run)
    Fire
    Right trigger, or left mouse once the view has the mouse
    Swap gun
    Y, or Q (Lancer / Gnasher)
    Reload
    RB, or E. Press again in the zone for an active reload (white = perfect: faster and more damage; grey = good: faster; miss = jam)
    R
    Reset

    What's real here

    Movement speeds, timings, reach and which moves chain come from Gears 3's code. The camera's field of view, shoulder offsets, follow lag and look speed come from Gears 3's camera settings; its pivot height and how it blends between modes are my approximations. Evade, slip and swat distances and timings come from Marcus's own animations. Vault timing and distance come from Gears 3's vault code and Marcus's jump animation; how far the far side of the wall is (a native function I can't read) is my approximation. Load your export to see him; without it you get a stand-in figure.
